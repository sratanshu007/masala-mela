1. Summary

Masala Mela is a puzzle game. You can play as a guest or log in with your mobile number (OTP) or Google account so your progress is saved to the cloud. The game shows ads (Google AdMob) and offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play or the Apple App Store.

2. Data we collect and why

  • Account details — the name you enter, and your mobile number (if you log in with OTP) or Google account email and name (if you log in with Google). Used to create your account, restore your progress on a new phone, prevent abuse and contact you about support requests.
  • Game progress — levels, stars, coins, boosters, lives, settings and purchase history in the game. Stored on your device and, when you are logged in, in our cloud database (Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore) so it is not lost.
  • Leaderboard — your display name, stars and level are visible to other players on the leaderboard. Your phone number and email are never shown to other players.
  • Game statistics — anonymous daily counts (for example levels played, ads watched, purchases made) used to balance the game and understand how it is doing.

Guests who do not log in: progress stays on the device only, except anonymous statistics.

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud). We keep account data while your account is active and delete it within 30 days of a deletion request.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to show rewarded video ads (only when you choose to watch one) and occasional full-screen ads between levels (never in the first 20 levels). AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA where permitted), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data, in order to deliver, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA/UK), we request your consent through Google's consent form. On iOS, personalised ads are used only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. We receive only a purchase confirmation so that the items you bought can be delivered in the game.

5. Children

Masala Mela is intended for a general audience aged 13 and above and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers named above (Google Firebase, Google AdMob, Google Play, Apple) for the purposes described.

7. Security and retention

Cloud data is protected by Firebase security rules so each player can access only their own account; data is encrypted in transit. Data processed by third-party providers is retained under their own policies.

8. Your rights and account deletion

Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct or erase your personal data and to withdraw consent. To delete your account and its data, email hello@deificdigital.com from the phone number's registered email or include your in-game name and number; we will confirm deletion within 30 days. For ad data, use your device ad settings or contact Google.

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do.
